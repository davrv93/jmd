Scope: web/index.html, the public raffle page for the Web scraping ético con IA workshop. Visitor mode: Persuade, mobile first.
Audience: people arriving from the flyer QR or WhatsApp, on a phone, with little time. Job: play once and keep the coupon. Action: name + WhatsApp → kick → code → canje.
Constraints: everyone wins 83 % (S/ 60 → S/ 10), exactly one person wins 100 % at random; closes today 5:00 p. m.; workshop 5:00–6:30 p. m. Only the essentials: no chat assistant, no long rules, no chips, no admin link. Help goes through WhatsApp 986 149 476.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the page is a single stadium ticket. You fill it in, you take your penalty and it comes back stamped with your coupon. It rejects the landing-page pattern of a hero with chips, stats and cards.
OWN-WORLD: the flyer's night navy #0b1030, the photo of the 3D penalty fading into it, and gold for "ético", the price and the main button. The ticket is bone-colored paper (#f3f1ea) with a die-cut line (dashes plus half-moon notches at the sides) that separates the header from the stub. Archivo Black is the display face. IBM Plex Mono is used only for ticket data: time, gate and code. The stamp is red #e10600, circular and tilted, like an ink seal.
STORY: in three seconds the visitor knows the workshop drops from S/ 60 to S/ 10, everyone wins 83 %, one person gets it free, and the raffle closes at 5:00 p. m. They believe it because nothing is hidden. They fill in two fields, kick, copy their code and go to the canje.
FIRST VIEWPORT (390×844): penalty photo at the top (~34 % of the height), fading into navy. The ticket overlaps it from below: title, struck-through price and a large S/ 10 under it, then the line "Todos ganan 83 % · 1 se lleva el taller gratis". Below the die-cut line comes the stub with name, WhatsApp and the gold "Patear mi penal" button, visible without scrolling.

SIGNATURE: when you submit, the stub turns into the goal, you choose where to aim (left, center, right) and it goes in. The ticket then gets the red stamp with its code (scale 1.5→1, tilt, a short thud). The 100 % winner gets the gold stamp with GRATIS.
